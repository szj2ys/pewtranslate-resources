A practical welcome-team workbook

Plan what every language can follow.

Map language support across a church service, name who will prepare it, and make gaps visible before guests arrive. This free planner creates a CSV and a printable handoff. It does not translate text or verify that support works.

Start with the support you can actually offer

  1. Ask about needs. Confirm preferred languages and usable formats with participants; do not infer them from appearance or nationality.
  2. Map each segment and language. Include arrival, songs, the sermon, announcements, and departure. A printed summary and full interpretation are different offers.
  3. Confirm with people, then rehearse. Record a responsible role, the exact reviewed material or interpreter, and a fallback. Keep incomplete support unconfirmed and tell the welcome team what remains unavailable.

This is an AI-assisted planning aid, not a field-tested ministry program. A “confirmed” choice records your team's judgment; the tool cannot assess translation quality, accessibility, or preparedness.
