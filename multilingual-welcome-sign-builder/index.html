PewTranslate · a practical welcome resource

A clear welcome, in your own words.

Create one printable sign with up to three language lines. Paste wording your team has reviewed, choose its reading direction, and print or keep an offline copy.

Your wording stays in this page’s memory. No uploads, storage, analytics, or account. Refreshing closes your work; download it first. This AI-assisted tool arranges text—it does not translate or check language accuracy.

1. Add your wording

All fields are optional, but a sign needs a title or a language line. Include only welcome information or assistance you can actually offer.

2. Add up to three lines

One reviewed phrase per field, up to 120 characters each. Leave unused fields empty. Direction controls reading order, not translation.

3. Set the page and review

Choose A4 in the print dialog. Turn browser headers and footers off and check the preview before printing. You can also choose “Save as PDF.”

Your sign

A4 · portrait · 210 × 297 mm

Add wording to start your sign.

Black text on white paper. Font size adapts to fit; the preview is scaled to your screen. Check the printed sign at its intended viewing distance. Long text is usually clearer as a separate handout.

Before you put the sign up

Ask people which language and format help them. Have a competent speaker review each phrase, then check the printed result together. A welcome sign should not promise interpretation, translated material, or other support that is unavailable.
